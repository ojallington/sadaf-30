Dress the house

Diergaarde Blijdorp

Blijdorplaan 8, Rotterdam · 10 min walk from Rotterdam Centraal

Sadaf's pick. Gorillas, the shark tunnel, penguins behaving badly. Everyone buys their own day ticket — but wait until the date is settled before you book. Already have a Blijdorp pass? Say so and skip this bit. We walk to the house together afterwards.

18:00 → 19:30 · the hop

Blijdorp to Schiedam is about twenty-five minutes door to door. Come straight on with us from the zoo — there's room at the house to change, so wear something you can walk a zoo in and bring the purple and gold in a bag. Not coming to Blijdorp? Just turn up at 19:30.

19:30 — late

The party

Noordvestsingel 81, 3119 DC Schiedam

Food, drinks, music and thirty candles. Two ways in: drift back with the zoo crowd from about 18:45 and change here, or arrive at 19:30 dressed and ready. There's a nazar over the door, so nothing can go wrong. Bring yourself; if you want to bring something, make it a bottle.
