۲15:00 — 18:00

Diergaarde Blijdorp

Blijdorplaan 8, Rotterdam · 10 min walk from Rotterdam Centraal · meet inside, at one exhibit

The surprise. Oscar walks her in thinking it's a quiet afternoon out; you are all already waiting at one particular exhibit, in purple and gold, among the animals. She finds out she has company when she sees it. Then gorillas, the shark tunnel, and the penguins, who will be better dressed than you whatever you do.

Everyone buys their own day ticket, but not yet: book before the poll lands and you may own a ticket to an afternoon at the zoo by yourself. The exhibit to meet at follows in the group. Got a Blijdorp pass? Say so in your reply and skip the ticket.

18:00 → 19:30 · the hop

Blijdorp to Schiedam is about twenty-five minutes door to door. At the gate, Oscar will casually suggest a few people come back for food and a drink. Everyone comes back. You'll land at the house around 18:45 and can change there, or arrive at 19:30 already in purple and gold. Either way, wear something you can walk a zoo in and bring the good outfit in a bag. Nobody has ever regretted the bag.

The party

Noordvestsingel 81, 3119 DC Schiedam

In Sadaf's mind: a few people back for food and a drink, nothing major. In reality: the house in purple and gold, music, food, and thirty candles on one cake, which under most fire codes is an event rather than a dessert.
